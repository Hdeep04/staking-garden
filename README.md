## About

### Staking is hard to understand

Many people who own stocks or funds find new crypto ideas confusing. Staking Garden makes them gentle and friendly.

- Made for people who hold stocks or mutual funds
- Great for older learners and young people new to crypto
- No trading and no game features
- Built around a calm bonsai world

### Learn, quiz, then try staking

Read a short card, answer a 3-choice quiz, and watch your garden grow. Then try a staking simulation.

- 6 learning cards about Ethereum staking
- Each right answer grows a pot in your garden
- Titles: Apprentice, Staker, Validator
- Staking trial unlocks at Staker; set amount, years, rate

### A safe first step into staking

The app uses no real money and no real blockchain. It is a learning tool, not investment advice.

- Simulation only: all numbers are sample values
- Japanese and English switch, plus a larger text option
- Forest green design with a bonsai feeling
- Next: make the garden grow in even more fun ways
